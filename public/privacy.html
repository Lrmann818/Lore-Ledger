Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Lore Ledger is a local-first campaign companion app designed to help you organize tabletop roleplaying campaign information, including campaigns, sessions, characters, notes, maps, images, drawings, combat information, and settings.

No accounts. No cloud sync. No backend.

Lore Ledger does not require an account. Lore Ledger does not provide cloud sync. Lore Ledger does not use a backend server for campaign data. Your campaign data never leaves your device unless you choose to export a backup yourself.

Data stored on your device

Lore Ledger stores user-created app data locally on your device or browser using standard browser and app storage technologies, including localStorage and IndexedDB. This may include campaign data, character data, session notes, map images, portraits, drawings, combat information, settings, and related app information.

This data is stored entirely on your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Device backups, such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup, may include this data. Those backups are managed by you and your device or browser provider, not by Lore Ledger.

Backups and exports

You may choose to export or import backup files. Exported backup files are files you create and control. If you share a backup file with someone else, that file may contain the campaign information, notes, images, drawings, characters, maps, settings, or other data you added to Lore Ledger. Lore Ledger does not receive or have access to your exported backups.

Data loss

Because Lore Ledger is local-first, your data may be lost if you clear browser or site data, delete the app, change devices, change browsers, reset your device, or otherwise remove local app storage. Export backups regularly if you want to preserve your data outside of the current device or browser.

Analytics, tracking, and third-party services

Lore Ledger does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, telemetry, or crash reporting. The app does not send your campaign data to Lore Ledger or to any third party.

This website

The web version of Lore Ledger at lore-ledger.com is hosted on GitHub Pages and delivered through Cloudflare. To load and protect the site, GitHub and Cloudflare process technical information such as IP address, browser type, and the pages requested, and Cloudflare may set strictly necessary security cookies. Lore Ledger itself does not set tracking or advertising cookies. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement and Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy.

The iPhone and iPad version runs from files included in the app.

Support requests

If you use Report Bug or email support, you choose what to send. Report Bug pre-fills an email with debug details such as the app version, current page, and browser or device capability information. It does not include campaign names or campaign content. Email sent to lore-ledger.com addresses is routed through Cloudflare Email Routing to the inbox used for support. That information is used only to respond to your request and fix problems.

Children's privacy

Lore Ledger is not directed to children under 13. It does not require accounts and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Users should avoid entering sensitive personal information into campaign notes or backup files.

Do Not Track

Lore Ledger does not track you over time or across third-party websites or apps, so browser Do Not Track signals do not change how it works.

About Lore Ledger

Lore Ledger is an independent app and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Wizards of the Coast, Hasbro, or any official publisher of tabletop roleplaying games.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes materially, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a revised date.
